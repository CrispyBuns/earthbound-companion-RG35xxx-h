# EarthBound achievements

Dev.34 adds 46 original offline achievements covering the journey from Onett
through the staff credits. Open **Achievements** in the launcher to browse Story,
Sanctuaries, Exploration and Battles, see progress and unlock dates, or turn
tracking off for the next launch. No account, server or separate application is
needed. Cheats and boosted settings are allowed; this is a casual local set.

Earned achievements stay earned when you reload an older phone save or quick
state. Replayed victories count toward battle goals. Photos count distinct
photographer locations, so repeating one photo does not fill the album. Level
progress remembers the highest level reached by any party member.

## Existing progress

The five-achievement pilot upgrades automatically, retaining its IDs, progress
and exact unlock dates. New battle goals inherit the minimum number of victories
already recorded; they cannot reconstruct unrecorded past battles.

An existing game save quietly earns credit for completed story milestones whose
flags still exist, plus recorded photos and character levels. Temporary story
flags can clear later, so not every earlier action can be reconstructed.
Starman Junior, Mini Barf and Kraken require an observed victory. Reaching the
credits from a completed save can credit the ending. Keeping your game saves and
previous achievements does not require another full playthrough.

## Storage and editions

Progress lives in **UserData/Achievements**, separate from phone saves and quick
states. Keep this directory when moving or updating an installation. Original,
Redux, Shuffle Original and Shuffle Redux have separate profiles. Shuffle seeds
share achievement progress within their edition; their game saves remain separate.

Unreadable, locked or unsupported profiles are preserved, and the game runs
without tracking for that session. Close any other game using the same profile
before retrying. Older pilot executables cannot read the expanded profile; keep
the updated executable with your progress.

The owner earned all five pilot achievements in normal play. The expanded set
passes focused Original/Redux production-hook tests, copied-save reconciliation,
profile upgrades and notification bounds checks. These tests cover all definitions;
they are not another natural campaign or certification of every seed/platform.
No RetroAchievements implementation or badge artwork is included.
