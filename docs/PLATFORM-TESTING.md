# Short platform preview tests

No second campaign is required. Use a new adventure or copies of your own saves. Report the platform, OS version, CPU architecture, build, game edition, display resolution and controller model. Include `game.log` from the selected adventure when relevant; do not upload ROMs or generated asset packs.

- **Setup and launch:** set up Original from your own clean USA ROM on desktop or Android. Repeat with Redux; on Android its source download needs internet. Keep the app open while building. Start the game and confirm text, sound and controls. Existing pack import remains available.
- **Movement:** walk, hold Y to sprint, and try sprint plus a Skip Sandwich. Visit a town and a crowded outdoor map, using 4:3 and widescreen. Watch scrolling, enemies at the sides and sprite clipping.
- **Battle and transitions:** enter a normal battle, cast a single-target PSI ability, exit, and use a door or teleport. Existing copied Giygas/credits saves can target those graphics without a full campaign.
- **Settings:** open settings from the game, change one setting, save and return. Confirm the game resumes and the option applies.
- **Saves:** make a phone save, export it, back it up, and check a copied restore. Keep the original safely outside the preview. Confirm Original, Redux and seeds have separate adventures.
- **Android lifecycle:** quick-save, open another app, return, lock/unlock, rotate and use Back. Confirm no held movement remains after interruption. Export a phone save before uninstalling. Check Bluetooth/USB controls if available.
- **Android handheld overlay:** in Controls, turn off Show touch controls and save. Confirm all touch buttons disappear, Android Back reveals them temporarily, and Settings can still be opened. Return to the game and restart the app; the hidden choice should persist. Re-enable it for touch-only play.
- **Android sliding controls (preview.19):** keep one thumb on the stick and slide right → up → diagonal without lifting. Hold Y / Run with another finger, then lift that finger while continuing to move. Try returning to the center, lifting, opening another app and hiding the overlay; none should leave an action held. Check both phone and tablet layouts, including larger system text.
- **Music and performance:** verify normal and MSU music if you use it, volume controls and fast-forward. Note stalls and audio interruptions with the device/build details.

For a useful report: state what you did, what happened, what you expected, whether it repeats, and the smallest save or recipe that reproduces it. Normal phone saves and recipes are preferred over raw quick saves from another engine build.
